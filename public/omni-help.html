Using Omni

Quotes, payments and order actions.

Quote to order

  1. Products & feesCheck lines and quantities
  2. Quote MemoChoose the correct wording
  3. Save As DraftSave the current quote
  4. HubSpot DealComplete the HubSpot step
  5. Download / convertCheck the result in Omni

Request Quote Review where required. Internal review approval is not Omni authorisation or invoicing.

Quotes & documents

  1. Check products, quantities, installation fees and freight.
  2. Click Quote Memo and review its wording, then Save As Draft.
  3. Use HubSpot Deal. Read any success or error message before continuing.
  4. Download the quote or convert to a sales order when the prerequisites are complete.

The HubSpot button requires the saved draft and Quote Memo steps. Existing dates and saved values are not replaced just by opening Help.

Payments & Send as

  1. Enter and check the payment on the correct NZSO.
  2. Choose the correct rep and review the WeCom message.
  3. Use Save & Send to WeCom. The helper saves and checks the payment before sending.
  4. Check the saved order total, paid amount and balance.

Payment comes from Omni amounts. Full payment changed to part payment returns HubSpot to Part Paid; removing the payment returns it to Wait On Customer unless an active site visit applies. Closed deals stay closed.

Freight, stock & packing

  • Ship freight routes unfinished goods to Delivery.
  • Collect freight routes unfinished goods to Collect.
  • Fully Packed - WMS turns the packed indicator green.
  • Green delivery-card shading requires Paid + In Stock + Packed.

Save freight and stage changes. Do not mark unavailable or backordered goods as delivered just to proceed.

Delivery & collection

  • Use Part Delivered when goods are still outstanding.
  • Use Delivered / Collected only when the goods are actually fulfilled.
  • Reverse Collected to Ready to Collect when needed; untick Picked & packed to return to the queue.
  • Keep the matching Omni order open. Workflow updates its stage and dispatch date/time in either direction; save in Omni to retain the changed fields.

Omni Fully Packed also reverses dispatch to readiness. Full completion fills Fully Dispatched, not Invoice Date. Delivery alone does not complete installation work.

Cloning & progress

  • Use Clone Quote once and wait for the new record to open. Check the new NZSO and quote lines.
  • The progress strip shows the current tracked job, including site visit, review and installation confirmations.
  • Jobs Overview opens all permitted jobs; filter quotes or sales orders and use Photos for the customer's images.
  • Quotes enter Workflow only after Site Visit or Quote Review is requested. A quote stays a quote until converted in Omni.

Workflow remembers your last planner per user. For errors or unsaved changes, record the NZSO and message using Report Issue; do not repeatedly click a busy action.

Photos & references

  • Link customer photos to the correct customer album and NZSO; search by customer, NZSO or rep.
  • Use Upload Photos / View Album to inspect the linked photos.
  • Baltic Manual and Motorised are separate custom products with shared MOQ and colour notes. Check their different SKUs and prices.
  • Staff Guide and Warranty sit beside Report Issue at the top. Warranty offers Quick Summary, Detailed Guide and the PDF.

Report any sales, stock, product, production, assembly, delivery, installation, customer-service or system issue. Check the prefilled customer/NZSO. Issue-review access is enabled per staff member in Admin.
